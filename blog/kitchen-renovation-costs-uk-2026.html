Kitchen Renovation Costs UK 2026

24 October 2026

Dreaming of a new kitchen but worried about unexpectedly high renovation bills? If you’re planning to update your kitchen in the United Kingdom in 2026, understanding the real costs is crucial. From modest makeovers to designer installations, this guide helps UK homeowners navigate kitchen renovation expenses with confidence.

Quick answer: According to MyJobQuote’s UK cost guide (updated October 2026), a full new kitchen in the UK, supplied and installed, typically costs about £6,000 to £20,000 or more: roughly £5,000–£7,000 for a budget kitchen, £8,000–£14,000 for mid-range and £20,000+ for high-end. Prices vary by size, quality, and location.

Key Takeaways

  • A mid-range kitchen typically costs about £8,000–£14,000 supplied and fitted (MyJobQuote, 2026).

  • Budget kitchens start at around £5,000–£7,000, while high-end projects can pass £20,000.

  • Which? estimates that labour makes up about 20–30% of the final spend if you don’t do any of the installation yourself.

  • Regional kitchen renovation costs UK differ, with London and the South East typically higher than Scotland or Wales.

  • Careful kitchen renovation financial planning UK can help you achieve a cost-effective upgrade.

What Affects Kitchen Renovation Costs UK 2026?

Size and Layout of Your Kitchen

One of the biggest factors in kitchen renovation costs UK 2026 is the size and configuration of your space. Larger kitchens need more units and worktop area, which increases cost. Reconfiguring walls, moving plumbing, or adding an island also raises kitchen redesign costs UK. For a standard 12m² kitchen, expect a mid-range remodel to fall within the national average, while bigger or open-plan kitchens see higher prices.

Quality and Specification Choices

The materials and appliances you select play a major part in the cost of kitchen upgrades in the UK. Budget options (laminate worktops, flat-pack cabinets) keep costs down, while quartz, solid wood, or bespoke cabinetry push prices up. Upgrading to energy-efficient appliances or smart tech can add value but increases upfront spend. For other big-ticket home investments, the principle is similar: specification drives price.

Breakdown of Average Kitchen Renovation Price UK

Labour and Installation Costs

Kitchen installation costs in the UK are a significant portion of your budget: Which? estimates labour at about 20–30% of the final spend, and MyJobQuote puts typical labour-only fitting at around £1,700 to £3,000. This includes fitting cabinets, worktops, appliances, electrics, plumbing, tiling, and sometimes plastering or decorating, and costs rise for complex or high-end projects, especially in areas like London or Manchester.

Materials, Appliances, and Fittings

Cabinetry makes up the bulk of materials spend, and premium brands or bespoke units cost far more than basic ranges. Worktops vary widely too, from laminate at the lower end to granite or quartz at the top. Don’t forget sinks, taps, and handles, or the appliance package (oven, hob, fridge, dishwasher, hood). Carefully planning your kitchen refurbishment budget UK helps avoid surprises.

Regional Kitchen Renovation Costs UK

London, South East and Major Cities

Kitchen renovation expenses are typically higher in London, Surrey, and areas like Oxford or Bristol, as labour rates, demand, and logistics push prices up. Get several quotes and check if VAT is included, especially with larger firms.

Wales, Scotland and Northern England

Homeowners in areas like Cardiff, Glasgow, Newcastle, or Liverpool can often access more affordable kitchen renovations UK. Labour rates are lower, and some suppliers offer regional discounts, though material choices and complexity still play a large role. Cost-Effective Kitchen Improvements UK

Upgrading without a Full Remodel

If a total renovation is out of reach, targeted kitchen upgrades can transform your space for less. Replacing cabinet doors, swapping out handles, fitting new worktops, or updating lighting costs a fraction of a full refit. Painting, vinyl flooring, and clever storage solutions further stretch your kitchen refurbishment budget UK.

2026 Kitchen Renovation Trends UK

Popular trends influencing kitchen renovation costs UK 2026 include sustainable materials, energy-efficient appliances, and multi-functional islands or breakfast bars. Homeowners in the United Kingdom are also opting for open shelving, smart lighting, and bold splashbacks. Staying on trend doesn’t always mean spending more—many affordable kitchen renovations UK incorporate these features creatively. Kitchen Renovation Financial Planning UK

Budgeting and Managing Costs

Set a clear budget before starting, and include at least 10% contingency for surprises. List must-haves and areas to save. Get multiple quotes and check if prices include removal of the old kitchen, waste disposal, and VAT. Using a reputable installer is vital—check for membership of a recognised scheme such as TrustMark, read customer feedback, and make sure any gas or electrical work is done by registered engineers. For larger projects, staged payments protect both parties.

Finance, Grants, and Savings

Some UK banks offer home improvement loans, and credit unions can be cost-effective. Grants for energy-efficient upgrades may be available via local councils or gov.uk’s energy efficiency schemes. Always compare finance options, and consider the long-term value of your investment, especially if you plan to sell your home in future. Frequently Asked Questions

How much to renovate a kitchen UK 2026?

MyJobQuote’s UK cost guide (updated October 2026) puts a full new kitchen, supplied and installed, at about £6,000 to £20,000 or more, with mid-range projects around £8,000 to £14,000. Costs depend on size, quality, and regional labour rates, so get several local quotes.

What is the cheapest way to upgrade a kitchen?

Affordable kitchen renovations UK include painting cabinets, fitting new worktops, changing handles, and upgrading lighting. These improvements cost far less than a full refit and can make a big visual impact.

What’s included in kitchen installation costs in the UK?

Kitchen installation costs generally cover fitting cabinets, worktops, appliances, plumbing, electrics, and tiling. Always check quotes for hidden extras like removal of the old kitchen or waste disposal, especially in larger UK cities.

Are there regional differences in kitchen redesign costs UK?

Yes, regional kitchen renovation costs UK vary. London and the South East are typically more expensive than areas like Wales, Scotland, or Northern England, mainly due to higher labour and supply costs.

Can I finance my kitchen renovation in the United Kingdom?

Many UK banks and lenders offer home improvement loans. Some energy efficiency grants are available through gov.uk for qualifying upgrades. Compare all finance options and read the terms carefully before committing.
